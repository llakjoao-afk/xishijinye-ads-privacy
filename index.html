1. About this notice

Xi Shi Jin Ye Ads Manager is an application planned for internal use by Haikou Xi Shi Jin Ye Industry Investment Co., Ltd. (海口溪石谨业投资有限公司, the “Company”). It is intended to manage only the Company's own authorized Amazon advertising accounts. It is not offered as a service for unrelated advertisers.

The application is under development. This notice describes its intended data handling when Amazon Ads API access is enabled. It does not imply that API access has already been approved or that data collection has begun.

2. Information the application will process

Within the permissions granted by the account holder and the capabilities enabled for the application, processing will be limited to information necessary for internal advertising operations:

  • Advertising account and profile identifiers, marketplace, currency and time zone.
  • Campaigns, ad groups, advertised products, keywords, targeting settings, bids, budgets and delivery status.
  • Advertising performance reports, such as impressions, clicks, spend and aggregate attributed sales or conversions.
  • Authorization tokens and limited operational logs needed to maintain the authorized connection and diagnose errors.

Authorization takes place through Amazon. The application will not ask for or store an Amazon account password. Individual shoppers' names, delivery addresses and payment details are outside the application's intended scope.

3. How information will be used

Information will be used to produce internal reports, analyze advertising performance and carry out authorized campaign, bid and budget management for the Company. Access and processing will remain subject to the applicable Amazon Ads API agreements and policies.

4. Access and disclosure

Access will be restricted to authorized Company personnel who need it for these purposes. Advertising information will not be sold, published, disclosed to unrelated advertisers or used to build services for other companies. API data will not be disclosed to outside parties except where required by law or expressly permitted by the applicable Amazon agreements and the relevant authorization.

5. Security and retention

Before production processing begins, the Company will implement safeguards appropriate to the data, including encrypted connections, protected storage of authorization credentials and access controls. Credentials and advertising data will not be included in the public website or its source repository.

Information will be retained only as long as necessary for the stated purposes and applicable obligations. It will be deleted when no longer required, following valid deletion requests and any mandatory Amazon deletion instructions. Any legally required retention will be limited to the information and duration required.

6. Authorization and requests

The account holder may revoke the application's authorization through Amazon's account controls. Revocation stops future access once Amazon applies the change; it does not by itself delete previously retained information. Questions or requests concerning access, correction or deletion can be submitted using the contact details below.

7. This notice page and updates

This static notice page contains no application login, data-entry forms, advertising scripts or analytics scripts. The website hosting provider may process connection information under its own privacy terms.

This notice will be updated when the application's data handling changes. The published version will identify its last update date.
